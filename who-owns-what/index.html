◎ Who Owns What?
Discover About

Who Owns What? is an open, source-first transparency reference. Every figure links back to a primary public source — SEC filings, FEC and OpenSecrets records, court and regulator databases, and official company reports.

This is a public-interest demonstration built on a curated sample dataset. Figures are approximate and illustrative — always confirm the current numbers at the linked source. Nothing here is investment advice.
